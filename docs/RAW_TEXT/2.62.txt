Union set for ordered lists with Thetha(n) runtime.

(define (union-set set1 set2)
    (cond ((or (null? set2) (null? set1)) (append set1 set2))
            ((= (car set2) (car set1))  (cons (car set1) (union-set (cdr set1) (cdr set2))))
            ((< (car set2) (car set1)) (cons (car set2) (union-set set1 (cdr set2))))
            (else (cons (car set1) (union-set (cdr set1)  set2)))
    )
)

TEST RESULT --- AFFIRMATIVE

> (union-set (list 1 2 3 4) (list 5 6 7 8))
'(1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8)
> (union-set (list 5 6 10 13) (list 5 9 10 11))
'(5 6 9 10 11 13)
> 

Is it a n runtime implementation? The number of steps will grow in these cases:
1. cdr down the two sets
2. Accumulate cons operations that are proportional to the number of elements in the sets.

So roughly the number of steps will be 4n which have an order of growth of thetha n.
