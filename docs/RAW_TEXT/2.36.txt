This exercise asks me to complete a procedure that performs accumulation in this order:
From a sequence of sequences, take the n-element of a nested sequence and perform the accumulation to the others n-elements in the other sequences:

(define (accumulate-n op init seqs)
    (if (null? (car seqs))
        '()
        (cons (accumulate op init (map (lambda (sub-seq) (car sub-seq)) seqs)) (accumulate-n op init (map (lambda (sub-seq) (cdr sub-seq)) seqs)))))
